How a floor works

You pay once

Pick a floor and a date. The price is set on chain from MON's real volatility, and the quote is what you pay.

The payout is locked

The most your floor could ever pay is locked in AUSD the moment you buy. Every floor is fully backed.

The vault hedges

A keeper shorts MON on Perpl through its API to offset the risk. A Chainlink CRE workflow checks the volatility input independently.

Settled on the date

Seconds after the date the floor settles at Perpl's oracle price. Below your floor, you collect the difference.

Or earn the premiums.

Put AUSD in the vault. It backs the floors people buy and keeps what they pay. When MON falls hard, payouts come out of the vault, so you can lose part of what you put in. The hedge on Perpl softens that, it does not remove it.

Resell any time.

Each floor is a token. On mainnet a Monday Trade pool already has real resales. On Kuru's on-chain order book, floors have traded on testnet, and Kuru is listing them on Spot V2.

What each piece does

PerplThe MON price, the settlement oracle and the venue the vault hedges on, through Perpl's trading API.
Chainlink CREA workflow computes MON's volatility from Perpl's candles and records it on chain as an independent check on the pricing input. It can also settle expired floors.
Agora AUSD + MeraFloors are paid and backed in AUSD. Your account is a passkey on your phone. No seed phrase.
Aurora IntentsAdd money from Base, Arbitrum, Ethereum and more. It lands in your Monad account in about a minute.
KuruFloors are a new asset class on Kuru's order book: a market and real fills on testnet, with the Spot V2 listing set up with Kuru's team.
EnvioIndexes every vault action and volatility update for the proof page.
