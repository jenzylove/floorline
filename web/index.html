How a floor works

You pay once

Pick a floor and a date. The premium is priced on chain from MON's real volatility, and the quote is what you pay.

The payout is locked

The most your floor could ever pay is locked in AUSD the moment you buy. Every protection token is fully backed. No promises, no leverage.

The vault hedges

A keeper shorts MON on Perpl through its API to offset the risk, and posts the volatility input. Chainlink CRE checks it independently.

Settled at the date

Seconds after expiry the series settles at Perpl's oracle price. Below your floor, you collect the difference. Above it, you kept the upside.

Or earn the premiums.

Put AUSD in the vault. It backs the floors people buy and keeps what they pay. When MON falls hard, payouts come out of the vault, so you can lose part of what you put in. The hedge on Perpl softens that, it does not remove it.

Resell any time.

Each floor is a token. Sell it before the date. On mainnet a Monday Trade pool already has real resales. On Kuru's on-chain order book, protection has traded on testnet, and Kuru is listing it on Spot V2.
