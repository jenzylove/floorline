The hedge on Perpl

The vault holds its own Perpl account. The keeper can only place orders through the vault, within 2% of Perpl's oracle, and the money never leaves the vault's control. Read from Perpl right now. Below it: the earlier fills, made through Perpl's trading API from the operator's account until 11 Oct.

A live market on Kuru Spot V2

FLOOR/USDC on Kuru's Spot V2 order book, a market Kuru's team created for Floorline on Monad testnet. A keeper reads the vault's price for a floor and keeps bids and asks resting around it, replacing them when the price moves or an order is filled. Read from the market contract right now.
